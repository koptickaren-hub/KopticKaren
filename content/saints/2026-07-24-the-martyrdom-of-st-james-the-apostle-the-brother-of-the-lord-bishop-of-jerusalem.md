He was known also by the just for the holiness of his life and his austere asceticism.</p><p>He was one of the twelve disciples of the Lord as St. Paul testified during his first visit to Jerusalem saying: \"Then after three years I went up to Jerusalem to see Peter and remained with him fifteen days. He had a strong personality which gave him a prominent position among the Jews on one side and his children the newly converted Jews to Christianity on the other side.</p><p>St. James presided over the first church council in the year 50 A.D. which was called the council of Jerusalem. This council debated the subject of judaizing the Gentiles who desired to enter the Christian faith. He had the final decision of the council. St. James wrote the epistle that is known by his name in the New Testament. St. Paul the apostle mentioned him as one of the three pillars of circumcision who \"gave me and Barnabas the right hand of fellowship that we should go to the Gentiles and they to the circumcised.\" (Gal. 2:9)</p><p>Many of the Jews believed at his hands during his episcopate which lasted about thirty years. They took him on the pinnacle of the temple to testify before the people against the Lord Christ.</p><p>When he refused they threw him down and he delivered his pure spirit and was buried nearby the temple of Jerusalem.   "reflection": "<p>St. James followed Christ, listened to His words, served His Church, and lived a life of prayer and holiness. He could not deny the One he had spent his life loving.</p><p>There is something beautiful about a heart that has become so rooted in Christ that fear cannot persuade it to become someone else. St. James reminds us that holiness is not only found in what we say during our greatest trials. It is formed through the quiet choices we make every day before those trials ever come.</p>",
